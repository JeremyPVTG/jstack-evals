- **Relational queries v2 is now the default.** Check any code that uses relational queries.

**Known issues:**
- **Open data-loss risk ([#5120](https://github.com/drizzle-team/drizzle-orm/issues/5120)).** When you change an enum, v1 generates a migration that drops and recreates the column. Hand-edit the generated migration before you apply it. The issue has 61 comments and is still open.
- **Fixed in 1.0.3 ([#5188](https://github.com/drizzle-team/drizzle-orm/issues/5188)).** Relational queries v2 returned the wrong nesting for self-relations. Don't go live on anything older than 1.0.3.

**Practitioner report:** [One team ran v1 in production for a month](https://blog.example-dev.io/drizzle-v1-in-production) (2026-10-18) and said it was "solid, but review every generated migration." I only got the headline, and it's a single source.

**Before you upgrade:**
1. Pin 1.0.3 or later and try it on a staging copy of your database.
2. Diff every generated migration, especially ones that touch enums.
3. Rerun your tests for relational queries, especially self-relations.
4. Change `push` to `sync` in your scripts.
5. Check your Postgres driver version.
