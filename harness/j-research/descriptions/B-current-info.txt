Use when the answer may have changed since training: current versions, prices and limits, best practice, which tools or services exist, whether something is production ready, how to build or choose something, or what people report using. Also for "find a way to build X", "what's best practice for Y", "is there something that already does Z". Not for editing code or writing text that needs no outside facts.
