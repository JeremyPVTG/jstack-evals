Use the j-scope skill to decide how much to build. I want uptime monitoring for my five side projects and I was planning to write my own checker with a dashboard, history graphs and a status page. Research brief (checked today): Better Stack free plan gives 10 monitors, 10 heartbeats, 1 status page, 3-minute checks. UptimeRobot free gives 50 monitors at 5-minute checks. Upptime runs on GitHub Actions and gives a status page in a repo. Writing my own would take about a weekend plus upkeep.
